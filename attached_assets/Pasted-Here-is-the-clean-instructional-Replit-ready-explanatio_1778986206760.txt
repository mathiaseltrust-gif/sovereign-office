Ancestry Family Tree Styles (Developer‑Ready Specification)

Ancestry uses three primary family tree styles, all of which are simply different visualizations of the same underlying data model (a directed acyclic graph of people and parent‑child relationships).

These styles matter because your Replit lineage engine must be able to import GEDCOM/CSV and render equivalent views.

---

1. Pedigree View — Horizontal Ancestor Tree

Purpose: Show direct ancestors only
Layout:

• Root person on the left
• Parents, grandparents, great‑grandparents extend to the right
• No siblings, no cousins, no descendants


Developer Notes:

• This is a strict ancestor chain
• Data required: person → parents
• Rendered as a binary ancestor expansion


Your engine equivalent:
SELECT * FROM ancestors WHERE lineage_direction = 'up'

---

2. Family View — Vertical Full‑Family Layout

Purpose: Show context, not just lineage
Layout:

• Person in the center
• Parents above
• Children below
• Siblings and spouses shown laterally


Developer Notes:

• Requires full relationship graph
• Includes: parents, children, spouses, siblings
• This is the closest to a complete household model


Your engine equivalent:
SELECT * FROM relationships WHERE person_id = X

---

3. Fan Chart — Radial Ancestor Expansion

Purpose: Show many generations of ancestors in a compact radial form
Layout:

• Person at the center
• Ancestors radiate outward in semicircular arcs
• Typically 4–7 generations


Developer Notes:

• Pure ancestor‑only view
• Same data as Pedigree, different geometry
• Requires generation indexing


Your engine equivalent:
SELECT ancestors ORDER BY generation ASC

---

Underlying Model (What Ancestry Actually Uses)

All three views are generated from the same structure:

A directed acyclic graph (DAG) of people and parent‑child edges

• Nodes = individuals
• Edges = parent → child
• No cycles allowed


This is the same structure used by GEDCOM, which is why Ancestry exports cleanly into GEDCOM files.

Replit Instruction Block — Ancestry Tree Styles

Our system must support the same family tree styles used by Ancestry. These are not different databases—just different visualizations of the same underlying lineage graph.

1. Pedigree View (Horizontal Ancestor Tree)

• Direct ancestors only
• Person on the left, ancestors expand to the right
• No siblings or descendants
• Data: parent relationships only


2. Family View (Vertical Full‑Family Layout)

• Person centered
• Parents above, children below
• Includes siblings and spouses
• Data: full relationship graph (parents, children, spouses)


3. Fan Chart (Radial Ancestor Expansion)

• Person in the center
• Ancestors radiate outward in arcs
• Pure ancestor‑only view
• Data: ancestor graph with generation indexing


All three views are generated from a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of individuals and parent‑child relationships.
Ancestry’s GEDCOM export follows this same model, so our importer must map GEDCOM individuals and family records into our Ancestor, ProtectedMember, and Relationship tables.
