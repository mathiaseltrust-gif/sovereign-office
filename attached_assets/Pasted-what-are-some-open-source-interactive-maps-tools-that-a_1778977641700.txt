There actually are some powerful tools out there — but they fall into different categories depending on what you’re trying to see:

territories + treaties
land loss timeline

I’ll show you the best ones, then the ones that come closest to what you’re specifically asking (timeline of dispossession + legal actions).

🧭 🏆 1. Best core interactive map (start here)
🌍 Native Land Digital (Indigenous‑led)
👉 https://native-land.ca

Interactive map of:
Indigenous territories
treaties
languages
native-land
+1
Search by address or region
Includes treaty layers and references
Why it matters:
It reframes land outside colonial boundaries
Shows overlapping territories (which is more accurate)
Limits:
Not a full legal timeline
Doesn’t track executive orders step-by-step

🌎 LandMark Global Platform
