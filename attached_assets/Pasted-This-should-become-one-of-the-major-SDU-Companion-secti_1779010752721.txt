This should become one of the major SDU / Companion sections because it connects:

* identity,
* bodily autonomy,
* spiritual practice,
* cultural continuity,
* family integrity,
* education,
* jurisdiction,
* and federal protections.

A strong title could be:

Indigenous Protective Rights Framework

or

American Indian Protective Rights & Continuity Protections

or more educational:

Protected Rights, Practices, and Continuity of Indigenous Peoples

The important thing:

do not present it as “special privileges.”

Present it as:

federally acknowledged protections tied to historical obligations, civil rights law, treaty relationships, constitutional protections, and continuity of Indigenous identity and practice.

⸻

Core Structural Categories

1. Identity & Self-Identification Protections

This section teaches:

* identity is not merely racial classification,
* Indigenous identity can involve:
    * community,
    * ancestry,
    * political relationship,
    * culture,
    * and continuity.

Possible subjects:

* self-identification
* tribal affiliation
* ancestry/community continuity
* anti-discrimination protections
* equal protection concepts

Useful authorities:

* Indian Civil Rights Act
* Morton v. Mancari

⸻

2. Religious & Ceremonial Protections

* gatherings

VERY IMPORTANT STRUCTURAL IDEA

Do NOT only show statutes.

Show:

1. the protection,
2. why it exists,
3. the historical harm it responded to,
4. and how it is applied today.

Example:

Protection	Historical Context	Modern Application
Hair protections	forced assimilation / boarding schools	school & workplace accommodation
ICWA	child removal	placement protections
AIRFA	suppression of ceremony	religious accommodation

That creates understanding instead of just legal citation dumping.
