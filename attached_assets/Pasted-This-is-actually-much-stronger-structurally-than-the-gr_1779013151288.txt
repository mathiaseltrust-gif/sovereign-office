Now the problems:

1. The Top Section Is Still Too Dense

This is the biggest formatting issue.

You currently have:

* return address,
* recorder use,
* court title,
* instrument title,
* subtitle,
* instrument date,
* title references,

all compressed together.  ￼

It visually collapses authority.

You need:

* more vertical spacing,
* stronger hierarchy,
* and a cleaner transition into the instrument itself.

[TOP RECORDING SPACE — EMPTY]
RECORDING REQUESTED BY:
[Return address block]
--------------------------------
RECORDER'S USE ONLY
--------------------------------
MATHIAS EL TRIBE SUPREME COURT
TRIBAL GRANT DEED

Tribal Sovereign Land Instrument

Right now everything feels “stacked.”

2. “Sovereign Land Instrument” Should Be Smaller

This line:

“Kern County, CA — Tribal Sovereign Land Instrument”

is visually competing with:

TRIBAL GRANT DEED

It should function as a subtitle, not a co-title.  ￼

⸻

3. Too Many Legal Assertions Inside the Granting Clause

This is extremely important.

The deed itself should primarily:

* convey,
* identify parties,
* identify property,
* identify trust structure.

Some of the jurisdictional assertions begin overwhelming the instrument itself.

For example:

“No state or county regulation…”  ￼

That reads more like:

* a declaration,
* notice,
* or sovereign position paper
    than a clean conveyance instrument.

You may want:

* the deed cleaner,
* and then attach:
    * “Appendix A — Sovereign Notice”
    * or “Notice of Jurisdictional Position.”

That separation increases credibility and readability.

⸻

4. The “Loper Bright Notice” Feels Forced Here

This section:  ￼

is intellectually interesting, but it feels inserted rather than organically connected to the function of the deed.

A recorder reviewing a deed expects:

* title,
* conveyance,
* parties,
* tax exemption basis,
* acknowledgment.

Not a jurisprudential interpretive framework.

That material may fit better in:

* supporting notice,
* memorandum,
* declaration,
* or attachment.

⸻

5. Signature Blocks Need Better Alignment

Right now the signature area visually drifts.  ￼

You want:

* clean columns,
* equal widths,
* signature lines,
* printed names directly underneath.

Example:

Grantor	Trustee
signature line	signature line
Printed Name	Printed Name
Date	Date

Cleaner. More official.

⸻

6. Notary Block Needs More White Space

The acknowledgment section is too compressed.  ￼

Notaries stamp heavily.
You need:

* more bottom margin,
* larger seal zone,
* wider line spacing.

Biggest Strategic Advice

This is the real key:

You are trying to put:

* philosophy,
* jurisdiction,
* governance,
* historical correction,
* and conveyancing

all into one document.

Instead:

Document	Purpose
Grant Deed	conveyance
Sovereign Notice	jurisdiction position
Trust Declaration	governance
Protective Covenant	land restrictions
Historical/Legal Memorandum	broader framework

Separating them actually makes the whole system stronger.

Because then:

* the deed looks cleaner,
* the notice looks intentional,
* the governance document looks authoritative,
* and the legal framework looks scholarly.

Right now they are partially blending together.
