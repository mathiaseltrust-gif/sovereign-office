2. Technical Federal Indian Law Meaning

Under mainstream federal Indian law doctrine, “restricted fee land” is usually treated as a specific legal category involving:

* fee title held by a tribe or Indian owner,
* plus federally imposed restrictions on alienation.

Courts often look for:

* congressional restriction,
* treaty-based restriction,
* federal statutory restriction,
* or formal federal trust/restricted status.

That is the distinction courts frequently make between:

* a privately imposed trust restriction,
    versus
* federally recognized restricted-fee status.

So legally, the debate is not whether restrictions exist — they do in your documents —
but whether those restrictions are recognized by federal/state institutions as carrying the same legal effect as classic federally restricted Indian land.
