If county or coordinates are missing, mark it as needs_review instead of discarding it.

Examples:
Bakersfield, CA → Bakersfield, Kern County, California
Southfield, MI → Southfield, Oakland County, Michigan

Atlas Mode Logic:
Atlas Mode must compare ancestors with historical events.

For each ancestor, calculate:
- whether the ancestor was alive during the event
- whether the ancestor had a known location during the event period
- whether the ancestor was within the affected region
- whether the ancestor had records matching the policy type, such as census, public school, health, land, county/state, migration, or classification records
