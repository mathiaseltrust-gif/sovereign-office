1. National Indian Law Library (NILL)

2. Kappler’s Indian Affairs: Laws & Treaties (GovInfo)

GovInfo hosts the digitized, official federal treaties and statutes governing American Indians.
Includes:

• All U.S. treaties with tribes (1778–1883)
• All federal laws & executive orders affecting tribes (1871–1970)
• Hosted by the U.S. Government Publishing Office with permanent public access  GovInfo


This is the canonical source for treaty rights.

---

3. Tribal Treaties Database (Oklahoma State University)

• Key provisions
• Indexes by tribe
